## What to do with it

Work through the twelve questions with whoever actually knows the answers, which is usually a mix of someone technical, someone commercial, and whoever fields customer security questions. It takes about ten minutes.

If more than three or four of them give you pause, that's the normal starting position rather than a red flag. Almost nobody has this mapped before someone external forces the question.
