## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the website owner.

## Cookies We Use

This website does not currently use any cookies other than those that are strictly necessary for the site to function. No analytical, tracking, advertising or functionality cookies are set.

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are essential for the website to operate. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

## Embedded Content

Some pages embed video hosted by YouTube. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which does not set tracking cookies when the page loads. If you choose to play a video, YouTube may store data on your device and will receive your IP address and playback information, governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) rather than ours. You can avoid this entirely by not playing the video.

## Managing Cookies

You can enable, disable, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. For guidance, visit [aboutcookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org) or consult your browser's help documentation.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our use of cookies changes. The date at the top of this page will reflect when it was last revised.
